BT CarPlay KeepAlive

Version 0.2.2

The Bluetooth device switch now appears

The Settings controller keeps its own specifier list. Earlier builds added the switch to a detached copy, so the visible Bluetooth device page did not show it. Version 0.2.2 updates the controller's stored list.

The switch was confirmed on an iPhone 11 running iOS 18.5. Turning it off and on rewrote the per-device preference as expected.
