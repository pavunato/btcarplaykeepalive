BT CarPlay KeepAlive

Keep your Bluetooth tethering active while CarPlay is on

v0.2.2 iPhone 11 / iOS 18.5 tested Rootless

Choose the Bluetooth device you use for tethering. While it is connected and CarPlay is active, a tiny packet gives the existing link a reason to stay busy. When either ends, the heartbeat stops.

Add to Sileo Release notes
One device at a time

The switch lives in Bluetooth Settings

Open Settings, tap the info button next to your tethering device, and enable “Stay Connected While CarPlay.” Each device keeps its own choice.

Traffic stays on its route

A heartbeat for the active link

Each four-byte UDP packet is bound to an active non-Wi-Fi interface, so it cannot quietly switch to the phone’s ordinary Wi-Fi or cellular route.

A quick status check

See when it is active

The optional CarPlay sidebar indicator is green after a heartbeat is sent and red when inactive. You can hide it from the same Bluetooth detail page.

Ready for the next drive.

For an existing Bluetooth tethering connection. The tweak does not pair or reconnect devices and cannot guarantee an accessory or carrier keeps the link open. Tested on iPhone 11 / iOS 18.5 with a rootless jailbreak.
