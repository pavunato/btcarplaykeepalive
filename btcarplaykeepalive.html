Made for the moments that move.

BT CarPlay KeepAlive works with your existing connection. It steps in only when CarPlay and your selected Bluetooth device are both active.

01 / CONTROL

One switch per device.

Find “Stay Connected While CarPlay” on the device’s Bluetooth detail page in Settings.

02 / PRECISION

Only during CarPlay.

The heartbeat stops when CarPlay ends, the device disconnects, or you turn the setting off.

03 / FEEDBACK

See the activity.

An optional CarPlay sidebar indicator turns green after a successful send and red when inactive.

Setup

Ready in a few taps.

No new app to manage. The control lives right where you already manage Bluetooth devices.

  1. Install the rootless package.Use your package manager or install the release `.deb` over SSH.
  2. Open Settings → Bluetooth → ⓘ.Choose the device you use for tethering.
  3. Enable Stay Connected While CarPlay.Connect to CarPlay and use your device normally.

Best-effort activity for an existing connection. It does not pair or reconnect devices, and cannot guarantee that an accessory or carrier keeps a link open. Built for rootless arm64/arm64e; private iOS APIs may change across releases.
